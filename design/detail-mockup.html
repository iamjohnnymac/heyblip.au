What this fix is about

Foreground reconnect coalescing

When the app came back from background, two reconnect requests were firing in the same millisecond — one from the lifecycle hook, one from the network observer. The fix adds a 250ms coalesce window so only the first one wins.

Testing: a real phone is the only way to prove this. The bug is timing-sensitive and won't repro reliably in the simulator. Three foreground cycles is enough to be confident.
